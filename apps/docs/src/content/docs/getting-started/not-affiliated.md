title: Not affiliated
description: How Sarraf relates to payment providers, and your responsibilities.
---

Sarraf is an independent open-source project. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by** Paymob, Kashier, Fawry or any other payment provider. Product names are used only to describe compatibility.

- **SDK, not a payment service.** You use your own provider accounts and keys. Money flows customer → provider → you. Sarraf never holds funds.
- **No card data.** Sarraf only uses hosted pages, redirects, tokens and reference codes. No API accepts a card number or CVV.
- **Your integration, your compliance.** You are responsible for following your providers' terms and applicable regulations.
- **No warranty.** Sarraf is licensed under Apache-2.0 and provided "as is".
